Time intelligence

MTD = month to date (here: the month total); YTD = year to date; SPLY = same period last year. In Power BI these are DAX measures (TOTALMTD, TOTALYTD, SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR).

What the data says

1 · 2017 is not down — the data is incomplete

Reported 2017 sales (505.4M) look 3.2% below 2016 (522.1M), but 2017 data stops in mid-December and two markets stop earlier. Comparing the same months (Jan–Sep), sales grew +8.6%; the five markets with a full 2016 base grew +3.7%.

2 · Revenue is concentrated in Singapore, San Francisco and New York

Singapore alone is 32% of sales, and the top three cities are 71%. Vancouver (+109%) and London (+38%) grow fastest but from a partial 2016 base. Paris (−14%) is the only established market that declined.

3 · Europe sells volume, the rest sells price

Rome and Paris ship 65% of all units but bring 20% of sales: their average price is 70–75% below list. Singapore's average price is 3.4× list. Before comparing markets, management should confirm whether sales amounts are in one currency.

4 · No single hero product

Each of the 8 products is 11–14% of sales. Alcohol leads by category (40%) because it has three products. Whisky, the most expensive item (list 4,000), carries the deepest average discount (42%).

5 · Booked sales and cash differ by a 12-day lag

Customers pay a median of 12 days after ordering. December 2016 was the strongest month by order date (58.6M, driven by Singapore at 2.1× its usual month), and 6.2M of 2017 orders are paid in 2018 — so finance (payment date) and sales (order date) reports will not match.

Recommendation

Report growth like-for-like, protect the three core cities, investigate Paris, and standardise currency and pricing rules across markets before setting discount targets. Next market launches: Copenhagen, Montreal, Hanoi are already set up in master data.

How it was built

1. Power Query (ETL)

  • Combined the 7 market files from the Facts folder in one query (Folder connector).
  • Derived the city key from the file name (NY_Results.xlsx → NY).
  • Removed 366 blank rows (London) and excluded a duplicate Singapore file in Other.

2. Star schema

One fact table (FactSales) with dimensions DimProducts, DimGeo and DimCalendar. The calendar has an active relationship on Order Date and an inactive one on Payment Date (role-playing dimension).

3. DAX measures

  • Total Sales, Total Units, ASP = Sales ÷ Units
  • Discount % = 1 − Sales ÷ Σ(Quantity × List Price)
  • MTD / YTD / SPLY with TOTALMTD, TOTALYTD, SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR
  • Sales by Order / Payment Date with USERELATIONSHIP
  • A dynamic measure (SWITCH) driven by a disconnected indicator table

4. Report

Navigation page, sales dashboard with bookmarks and a report-page tooltip, discount page, time-intelligence page. This web version recomputes the same measures in the browser from an aggregated extract (data.json).
